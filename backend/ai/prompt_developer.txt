## COMPREHENSIVE FIELD EXTRACTION RULES

### BALANCE SHEET EXTRACTION RULES

**TOP-LEVEL AGGREGATES (Mandatory Totals):**
- `totalAssets`: **EXTRACTION RULE**: Find the value where total assets information is present and extract the grand total of all assets. This must equal `totalEquityAndLiabilities`. Look for the final balance sheet total that represents the sum of all company assets.
- `totalEquityAndLiabilities`: **EXTRACTION RULE**: Find the value where total equity and liabilities information is present and extract the grand total. This must equal `totalAssets`. Look for the final balance sheet total that represents the sum of all equity and liabilities.

**NON-CURRENT ASSETS SECTION:**
- `nonCurrentAssets.total`: **EXTRACTION RULE**: Find the value where total non-current assets information is present and extract the sum of all non-current asset sub-fields. Look for the total that represents all long-term assets.
- `propertyPlantAndEquipment`: **EXTRACTION RULE**: Find the value where property, plant and equipment information is present and extract the net value after accumulated depreciation/impairment. Look for tangible fixed assets like land, buildings, machinery, etc.
- `rightOfUseAssets`: **EXTRACTION RULE**: Find the value where right-of-use assets information is present and extract the value. Look for assets from operating leases recognized under lease accounting standards.
- `capitalWorkInProgress`: **EXTRACTION RULE**: Find the value where capital work-in-progress information is present and extract the cost. Look for assets currently under construction or installation.
- `goodwill`: **EXTRACTION RULE**: Find the value where goodwill information is present and extract the value. Look for intangible assets arising from business combinations and acquisitions.
- `financialAssetsInvestments`: **EXTRACTION RULE**: Find the value where long-term investments information is present and extract the value. Look for investments in subsidiaries, joint ventures, associates, or other non-current portfolios.
- `financialAssetsLoans`: **EXTRACTION RULE**: Find the value where non-current loans information is present and extract the value. Look for loans given by the company to employees, related parties, or other entities.
- `otherFinancialAssets`: **EXTRACTION RULE**: Find the value where other non-current financial assets information is present and extract the value. Look for restricted cash, long-term trade deposits, derivatives, etc.
- `deferredTaxAssetsNet`: **EXTRACTION RULE**: Find the value where deferred tax assets information is present and extract the net value. Look for future tax benefits arising from temporary differences.
- `incomeTaxAssetsNet`: **EXTRACTION RULE**: Find the value where income tax assets information is present and extract the net value. Look for advance tax payments exceeding liability for non-current recovery.
- `otherNonCurrentAssets`: **EXTRACTION RULE**: Find the value where other non-current assets information is present and extract the value. Look for miscellaneous non-financial assets.
- `intangibleAssets`: **EXTRACTION RULE**: Find the value where intangible assets information is present and extract the value. Look for non-physical assets like patents, trademarks, software, or assets under development.

**CURRENT ASSETS SECTION:**
- `currentAssets.total`: **EXTRACTION RULE**: Find the value where total current assets information is present and extract the sum of all current asset sub-fields. Look for the total that represents all short-term assets.
- `financialAssetsInvestments`: **EXTRACTION RULE**: Find the value where short-term investments information is present and extract the value. Look for investments in liquid mutual funds or market instruments maturing within 12 months.
- `tradeReceivables`: **EXTRACTION RULE**: Find the value where trade receivables information is present and extract the net value. Look for accounts receivable outstanding from customers, net of impairment allowance.
- `cashAndCashEquivalents`: **EXTRACTION RULE**: Find the value where cash and cash equivalents information is present and extract the value. Look for cash in hand, bank balances, and highly liquid investments.
- `financialAssetsLoans`: **EXTRACTION RULE**: Find the value where current loans information is present and extract the value. Look for current portion of loans and short-term loans given by the company.
- `otherFinancialAssets`: **EXTRACTION RULE**: Find the value where other current financial assets information is present and extract the value. Look for accrued interest, short-term deposits, etc.
- `incomeTaxAssetsNet`: **EXTRACTION RULE**: Find the value where income tax assets information is present and extract the net value. Look for advance tax payments for the current period/recovery.
- `otherCurrentAssets`: **EXTRACTION RULE**: Find the value where other current assets information is present and extract the value. Look for prepaid expenses, inventory other than primary stocks, etc.

**EQUITY AND LIABILITIES SECTION:**
- `totalEquity`: **EXTRACTION RULE**: Find the value where total equity information is present and extract the value. Look for the total shareholders' equity (Share Capital + Other Equity).
- `equityShareCapital`: **EXTRACTION RULE**: Find the value where equity share capital information is present and extract the value. Look for the face value of issued and paid-up shares.
- `otherEquity`: **EXTRACTION RULE**: Find the value where other equity information is present and extract the value. Look for reserves and surplus, retained earnings, OCI, etc.

**NON-CURRENT LIABILITIES:**
- `nonCurrentLiabilities.total`: **EXTRACTION RULE**: Find the value where total non-current liabilities information is present and extract the sum of all non-current liability sub-fields. Look for the total that represents all long-term liabilities.
- `nonCurrentLiabilities.financialLiabilitiesBorrowings`: **EXTRACTION RULE**: Find the value where non-current borrowings information is present and extract the value. Look for long-term loans and debentures that capture the principal debt component.
- `nonCurrentLiabilities.financialLiabilitiesLease`: **EXTRACTION RULE**: Find the value where non-current lease liabilities information is present and extract the value. Look for the principal portion of ROU liability.
- `nonCurrentLiabilities.otherFinancialLiabilities`: **EXTRACTION RULE**: Find the value where other non-current financial liabilities information is present and extract the value. Look for derivatives, non-current deposits, etc.
- `nonCurrentLiabilities.deferredTaxLiabilitiesNet`: **EXTRACTION RULE**: Find the value where deferred tax liabilities information is present and extract the net value. Look for liabilities arising from temporary differences.
- `nonCurrentLiabilities.otherNonCurrentLiabilities`: **EXTRACTION RULE**: Find the value where other non-current liabilities information is present and extract the value. Look for long-term deferred revenue, statutory payables, etc.

**CURRENT LIABILITIES:**
- `currentLiabilities.total`: **EXTRACTION RULE**: Find the value where total current liabilities information is present and extract the sum of all current liability sub-fields. Look for the total that represents all short-term liabilities.
- `currentLiabilities.financialLiabilitiesBorrowings`: **EXTRACTION RULE**: Find the value where current borrowings information is present and extract the value. Look for short-term loans and current maturity of long-term debt that captures the short-term debt component.
- `currentLiabilities.financialLiabilitiesLease`: **EXTRACTION RULE**: Find the value where current lease liabilities information is present and extract the value. Look for the principal portion of lease liability due within 12 months.
